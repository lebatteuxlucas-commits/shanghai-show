Privacy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

The China Cycle 2026 Supplier Directory is published by FutureMotion Limited, Hong Kong. You can browse it without an account. This page explains what we collect and why.

Browsing the directory

We use no advertising or analytics trackers and set no third-party cookies.

Your notes, statuses, catalogue marks and edits are saved in your own browser (local storage) and never leave your device. During a visit, your browser also remembers which categories you filtered and which suppliers you opened; this is only sent to us if you submit a catalogue request, as described below.

The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes standard technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.

Catalogue request form

When you request a supplier catalogue, we collect:

  • your work email and company, and optionally your name and a message;
  • the supplier you asked about;
  • the categories you filtered and the suppliers you viewed during your visit;
  • the date and time of the request and the consent statement you accepted;
  • a one-way hash of your IP address, used only to limit abuse of the form (your IP address itself is not stored).

We use this to send you the catalogue, answer your request and, as you agreed, contact you about this request and sourcing in China. You receive a short email confirming we received your request.

Requests are stored in our database (Neon) and emails are sent through Resend. We do not sell your data or share it with the suppliers listed in the directory.

Contacting us about a supplier

The “Ask FutureMotion about this supplier” button opens your own email app. We receive whatever you choose to send, as with any email.
